# INKdown Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

INKdown is a note-taking app.

## What we access
If you choose to sign in with Google, INKdown asks for access to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope). It cannot see, read, or change any other file in your Drive.

## What we store
Your notes are stored on your device. If you tap backup, your notebook file is saved in that hidden folder in your own Google Drive. We do not have a server and we do not receive, copy, or store your notes or your Google data.

## Sharing
We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone.

## Your control
You can delete backups inside the app, sign out at any time, or remove INKdown's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access does not delete your notes on the device.
